Principles

Built with Integrity

We follow three foundational tenets in everything we design and code.

Privacy by Default

Your files and financial data belong to you. We store data directly on your device or in your personal Google Drive / Sheets. No surveillance, tracking pixels, or data brokering.

Offline-First Design

Software should work even when the connection fails. Our apps ensure core playback, tracking, and editing experiences remain fully functional without internet access.

Focused & Uncluttered

No subscription walls for basic features, no spam push notifications, and no confusing UI. We construct clean, intentional tools that do one job exceptionally well.
