Privacy Policy

Garden100 is designed with user privacy at its core. This Privacy Policy explains how our US stock portfolio monitoring application interacts with your Google Account and handles data.

What Information We Collect

When you use our Service, we may request access to your Google Account, including basic account information such as your name and email address, in order to provide Google Sign-In and core features of our Service.

Our app is a US stock portfolio monitoring application. To provide portfolio tracking and stock-related features, the app uses Google Sheets and Google Finance. Portfolio information and related records are stored in Google Sheets directly within your own Google Drive account.

We do not operate a database server for storing your portfolio holdings. Your portfolio data is stored securely in your Google Drive and is subject to Google's storage and security practices.

Our Service maintains a server-side database containing publicly available market and dividend information (such as historical payout schedules). This is used solely to provide dividend analysis features.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information and permissions provided through your Google Account solely to provide and operate the features of our Service, including:

  • Authenticating your session via Google Sign-In
  • Creating and managing Google Sheets used for your portfolio
  • Reading and updating portfolio entries stored in your Google Sheets
  • Querying Google Finance functions within your sheets for pricing data
  • Displaying your portfolio dashboard within the app
  • Supplementing stock tickers with dividend information from our public database

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or portfolio records to any third parties.

Google Drive and Google Sheets

Our Service uses Google Drive and Google Sheets to store your portfolio information. Your records reside in your Google Drive account rather than on our server infrastructure.

Because your data stays in your personal Google Drive, you retain complete ownership and can inspect, modify, export, or delete your files at any time.

How We Protect Your Information

Your portfolio data remains isolated in your Google Drive. We recommend protecting your Google Account by employing strong, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You may review and revoke permissions granted to Garden100 at any time through your Google Account security settings.

Retention and Deletion of Google Data

Since Garden100 does not copy your portfolio records to external servers, deleting your portfolio spreadsheet from Google Drive permanently removes the data.
