What Information We Collect

When you use our Service, we may request access to your Google Account, including basic account information such as your name and email address, in order to provide Google Sign-In and the features of our Service.

Our app is a US stock portfolio management application. To provide portfolio tracking and stock-related features, the app uses Google Sheets and Google Finance. Portfolio information and related data are stored in Google Sheets within your own Google Drive account.

We do not operate our own server for storing your portfolio information. Your portfolio data is stored in your Google Drive and is subject to Google's storage and security practices.

Our Service also maintains a server-side database containing publicly available stock and dividend information, such as dividend data associated with stock tickers. This information is used to provide dividend-related features within the app.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information and permissions provided through your Google Account solely to provide and operate the features of our Service.

This may include:

Allowing you to sign in using your Google Account.
Creating and managing Google Sheets used for your stock portfolio.
Reading and updating portfolio information stored in your Google Sheets.
Using Google Finance functions within Google Sheets to obtain stock market information.
Displaying your portfolio and related information within the app.
Providing dividend information for stock tickers using our server-side database.

We do not sell your personal information or portfolio information to third parties.

Google Drive and Google Sheets

Our Service uses Google Drive and Google Sheets to store and manage your portfolio information. When you use these features, your portfolio information is stored in your Google Drive account rather than in our server-side database.

The app may create, read, update, or otherwise interact with Google Sheets that are used for your portfolio. Google Finance may be used within these Sheets to retrieve stock-related information.

Because this information is stored in your Google Drive, you retain control over your files and can view, modify, or delete them through your Google Account and Google Drive.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by our application. Your portfolio information is primarily stored in your own Google Drive rather than on our servers.

The security of information stored in Google Drive is also subject to Google's security measures and your Google Account settings. You should protect your Google Account by using a strong password, enabling appropriate security features such as two-step verification, and keeping your account credentials confidential.

Our server-side database is used to store stock and dividend information needed to provide dividend-related features. We take reasonable measures to protect information maintained on our servers.

Google API Services

Our Service uses Google API Services to provide features involving your Google Account, Google Drive, and Google Sheets.

Our use of information received from Google APIs will comply with applicable Google API Services requirements, including the Google API Services User Data Policy. We only request access to Google data that is necessary to provide the functionality of the Service.

You can review and manage the permissions granted to our application through your Google Account settings. You may revoke access at any time. Revoking access may prevent certain features of the Service from functioning.

Retention and Deletion of Google Data

Your portfolio information stored through our Service is stored in your Google Drive account. We do not maintain a separate server-side database containing your portfolio information.

Our server-side database may retain stock and dividend information associated with stock tickers for as long as necessary to provide, maintain, and improve the dividend-related features of the Service. This information is generally not your personal portfolio data.

You can delete portfolio information or Google Sheets created by the Service directly from your Google Drive. You may also revoke the application's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account settings.

Please note that revoking access to the application does not automatically delete files that have already been created in your Google Drive. You can delete those files directly from Google Drive.
