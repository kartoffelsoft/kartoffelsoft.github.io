Garden100

Watch your financial garden grow with complete privacy.

Investing in stocks is like growing a garden. You plant your investments, watch them grow, and care for them over time.

Garden100 is a simple portfolio monitoring app for US stocks. Create your own portfolios, keep an eye on your investments, and enjoy watching your financial garden flourish.

Originally created for family use — one portfolio for me and one for my daughter — it is designed specifically for calm monitoring and learning, rather than active day-trading or buying and selling.

Your Portfolio, Your Data

Garden100 uses Google Sheets to store your portfolio information. Your portfolio data stays entirely in your own Google Sheet.

Our servers do not store your portfolio information. We only provide supplementary public market data that makes the app useful, such as dividend histories, company overviews, and ticker search.

🔒 Zero Data Harvesting: We never collect, sell, or store your portfolio data on our servers.

How It Works

Create a portfolio in Garden100. Your portfolio data is stored and kept securely inside your own personal Google Sheet.

Why Google Sheets?

We believe you should remain in complete control of your financial records. Storing your portfolio in your own Google Sheet instead of a proprietary third-party database means:

  • You can inspect, export, and edit your data anytime using a tool you already know and trust.
  • You aren't locked into our app or service.
  • If our server goes down, your records remain completely safe in your Google Drive.

What Does Our Server Do?

Garden100's server only supplies publicly available market metadata that is difficult to maintain inside a static spreadsheet:

  • Historical dividend distributions and yields
  • Company profile overviews and market sectors
  • Stock ticker search and discovery

Your private holdings, share amounts, and balances are never sent to or logged on our server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is this a real trading app?
No. Garden100 is purely a portfolio monitoring and tracking app. It does not execute stock trades or hold customer funds.
Q. Where is my portfolio data stored?
Your portfolio information is stored in your personal Google Sheet in your Google Drive. Garden100 does not store your holdings on its servers.
Q. Do you collect my personal financial records?
No. We never collect, harvest, or monetize your portfolio records.
Q. Why do I need Google Sheets?
Google Sheets gives you a reliable, free, and accessible home for your records while leaving you in 100% control of your data.
Q. Is Garden100 financial or investment advice?
No. Garden100 is an informational monitoring tool and does not provide financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Always do your own research or consult an accredited professional.
