Privacy Policy

At Kartoffel, we take your privacy very seriously. Our service offers a seamless experience by enabling you to download your music directly from the cloud onto your device. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our products and services.

Kartoffel Music's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Information We Collect

When you use our Service, we may request certain cloud login information such as your name, email address, and other contact details. This information is collected when you connect to Cloud in our service. We enable you to download from Cloud (e.g., Google Drive), and store your files directly on your device.

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to provide you a comfortable offline music play service. This includes allowing you to log in using your Google account and facilitating the download and storage of data from your Google Drive. Once downloaded, you can access your music collection even when you're offline, eliminating the need for a stable internet connection.

How We Protect Your Information

Your personal information and media files are stored only on your device, and we do not export your information outside of your device. We do not keep your information on external servers. While we take reasonable steps to protect your personal information, it is important for you to also take precautions to protect your data with strong device passwords.

Retention and Deletion of Google Data

Our service utilizes Google services and may process data through Google's APIs, SDKs, or other services. As our service integrates with Google Drive, we may request and store access permissions to your Google Drive files for the purpose of downloading your selected songs. You can revoke these permissions at any time through your Google Account security settings. We do not store the content of your files on external servers.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by an announcement on our app.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at kartoffelsoft@gmail.com.
