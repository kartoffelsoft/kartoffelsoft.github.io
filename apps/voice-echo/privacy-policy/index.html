Privacy Policy

At Kartoffel, we take your privacy very seriously. Our service offers tools to help you learn, revise, and retain information effectively through audio repetition. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our products and services.

What Information We Collect

When you use our Service, we may request basic login information such as your name and email address. This information is collected solely when you register or sign in.

How We Use Your Information

We use your login information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Account Creation & Management: To create and manage your account, provide secure access, and store your study preferences.
  • Authentication: To authenticate your access, ensuring the confidentiality of your study recordings and data.
  • Communication: To send important product updates and critical notifications.
  • Security: To protect against unauthorized access and maintain app reliability.

How We Protect Your Information

By default, your personal information is stored only on your device, and we do not export your information outside of your device without your consent. While we implement strict data protection measures, please maintain strong, unique passwords on your device.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through in-app announcements or via our website.
