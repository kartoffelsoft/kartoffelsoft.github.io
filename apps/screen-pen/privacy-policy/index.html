Privacy Policy

Kartoffel Screen Pen is a macOS screen annotation tool that allows users to draw and highlight over any application using mouse input and keyboard shortcuts. We respect your privacy and design the app so that it does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

Data Collection

Kartoffel Screen Pen does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do NOT collect:

  • Mouse input data
  • Keyboard input data
  • Screen content
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers

Accessibility Permission

The app requests Accessibility permission from macOS. This permission is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Detecting global mouse movement and clicks
  • Enabling drawing and annotation over other applications
  • Handling user-defined keyboard shortcuts

Accessibility access is only active when the user explicitly enables drawing mode (e.g. “Start”). When drawing mode is inactive, no input monitoring occurs.

We do not log, record, or transmit any input captured through Accessibility APIs.

How We Protect Your Information

Your personal information is stored only on your device and we do not provide a way to export your information outside of your device. That being said, we do not keep your information outside of your device. While we take reasonable steps to protect your personal information, it is important for you to also take precautions to protect your data. Ensure that you use strong, unique passwords on your device and keep them confidential.

Data Storage

Kartoffel Screen Pen does not store any user input or screen content. All drawing data exists only in memory during active use and is not persisted unless explicitly saved by the user.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by an announcement on our app.
