Privacy Policy

At Kartoffel, we take your privacy very seriously. Our service offers a focused, distraction-free environment to help you learn, revise, and retain information effectively. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our products and services.

What Information We Collect

When you use our Service, we may request basic login credentials such as your name and email address. This information is collected solely when you sign in to our service.

How We Use Your Information

We use your login information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Account Creation & Management: To create and manage your account, synchronize your decks, and personalize your study sessions.
  • Authentication: To authenticate your access, ensuring the security of your account and flashcards data.
  • Communication: To deliver critical service notices or policy updates.
  • Security: To prevent unauthorized access and safeguard your account integrity.

How We Protect Your Information

By default, your personal information is stored securely on your device, and we do not export your information outside of your device without your explicit consent. While we take reasonable technical steps to protect your personal information, please also take standard precautions such as maintaining strong passwords on your devices.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes via in-app notification or website announcement.
